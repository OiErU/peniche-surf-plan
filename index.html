Sources & method

Surfline and Surf-Forecast provide grades and physical conditions. SwellMap provides additional ECMWF support. 4Shor Surfing / Intermediate is a bounded supporting opinion for bodyboarding, never a replacement for local comfort and tide limits.

Quality is a calibrated index, not a probability. Personal preferences affect ranking separately. Provider disagreement lowers confidence. Missing optional evidence never becomes a zero grade.

Three-hour 4Shor windows retain their original resolution. Tide curves are estimates between published tide events, using that source’s height reference. Tide and swell conditions can differ across nearby breaks.

Day cards show only collected physical forecasts. A standalone 4Shor score cannot create a recommendation.
