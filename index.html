How forecast sources grade conditions

SwellMap (1–10)

Blends swell energy & wind. 7–10 Good (clean, offshore); 4–6 Average (surfable/variable); 1–3 Poor (messy/blown out). Algorithm cannot account for local reef wrap.

Surf-Forecast (0–10★)

Swell size & period set base stars; onshore wind drops stars and fades color to pale. Bright 10★ is classic big surf with light offshore; 0★ is flat or blown out.

Cam observations & wave models (Poor to Epic). Strictly penalizes onshore winds without factoring local break bathymetry.

Sources & method

Wind is powered primarily by IPMA AROME 2.5km (the Portuguese high-resolution model providing hourly u/v vectors and gusts), falling back to Surfline and Surf-Forecast when out of range.

Each card shows the source ratings for that session in their original scale. They are different assessments, not directly comparable percentages. Surfline and Surf-Forecast provide grades and physical conditions; SwellMap provides ECMWF support.

4Shor is Surfing / Intermediate, a supporting opinion for bodyboarding. Multi-block scores are marked as a window blend. Daily forecasts are used when recently collected and matched to the session date. The location update timestamp does not exclude them. Missing or incomplete windows still cannot contribute.

Our session index also considers your local spot knowledge, comfort limits, wind and tides. It is not a provider grade or a probability. Personal preferences affect ranking separately.

Photos show the location, not live conditions. Beachcam provides a live view, not a numerical rating. Tide curves use the selected source and station/grid; Open-Meteo total sea level is approximate. Heights retain their datum and are not local reef depth. IH observations are validation only. All times use Europe/Lisbon.
